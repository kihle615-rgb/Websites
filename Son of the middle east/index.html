The house

Fragrance is the shortest road back to a memory.

In the Middle East a fragrance is not an afterthought on the way out the door. It is offered to guests, layered for an occasion, and worn close enough that people remember you by it years later.

We bring that tradition to Gqeberha: oud, amber, rose and musk, built in the concentrations they are meant to be worn at. Nothing here is designed to disappear by lunchtime.

Come in and smell them properly. That is the only way to choose a fragrance, and it is why we would rather you visited than guessed from a screen.

    How a scent unfolds

    Top, heart, base.

    A fragrance is not one smell — it is three, arriving in order. What you smell on the card in the first ten seconds is the part that leaves first. This is why we ask you to wear it a while before deciding.

    1. 01

      Top

      First 15 minutes

      The opening. Citrus, pepper, fruit — bright, sharp, and gone quickly. It is the introduction, not the fragrance.

    2. 02

      Heart

      20 minutes to 2 hours

      The body of it. Rose, jasmine, spice. This is the character you will actually wear for most of the day.

    3. 03

      Base

      2 to 8 hours and beyond

      What stays. Oud, amber, musk, sandalwood — the part that settles into fabric and skin, and the part people remember.
