The house

A counter, a shelf,
and two hundred bottles.

Ghazi Oriental is the fragrance list at Son of the Middle East. Every bottle on it is a designer-inspired composition — the accords you already know from the department-store counter, rebuilt to sit close to the skin and stay there.

Nothing here is a secret. We tell you what a scent is modelled on, we sell it by the millilitre, and the price is the same whether the name on the shelf is famous or not.

  • Elegance

    Oud, rose, amber and musk, blended for weight rather than noise.

  • Class

    Nothing on the list costs more than R250. That is the whole point of it.

  • Identity

    Come in and try them on skin. A fragrance only tells the truth after an hour.
